Baking with a Toddler: Messy Hands, Big Skills and a Banana Nutella Swirl Bread

By Stacey Catlin, The Little Cup Cafe

Difficulty: Easy

Learning kitchen skills is one of the most valuable things any person can pick up, and we all know how stressful (and messy) a small person in the kitchen can be.

But ignore the mess, because it can be cleaned up. Ignore the extra egg that landed on the floor instead of in the bowl, because they’re learning a big skill and loving every minute of it, right up to the first taste of fresh banana Nutella bread.

There are no fancy tools in this bake, just a bowl, a fork and a whisk. It’s the perfect recipe for curious, ready-to-help little hands.

Banana Nutella Swirl Bread

Ingredients

Method

  1. Peel and mash the bananas until smooth. This is the perfect job for little hands with a fork.
  2. Add the eggs and yoghurt and mix until well combined.
  3. Melt the butter and stir in the sugar until dissolved. Add to the banana mixture and combine well.
  4. Mix the flour and baking powder together, then add to the wet ingredients. Mix only until combined. Do not overmix.
  5. Warm the Nutella slightly in the microwave so it’s easy to swirl.
  6. Line a 2lb loaf tin (approximately 23 x 13 cm) with baking paper. Pour in half the banana mixture.
  7. Dollop half the Nutella on top and swirl through with a skewer.
  8. Pour in the remaining banana mixture and repeat with the remaining Nutella, swirling again.
  9. Bake at 180°C (160°C fan-forced) for 60 to 70 minutes, until a skewer comes out clean.
  10. Cover with foil at the 30-minute mark to stop the top browning too much.
  11. Leave the bread in the tin to cool fully before slicing and serving.

Storage and Freezing

Keep covered at room temperature for up to 5 days. Alternatively, slice and freeze for up to 3 months, which makes a perfect grab-and-go snack.
